# Code of conduct

nirvor follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or publishing other people's private information.

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the
email on [@Muktaditbf's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Muktaditbf). Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments or block participants who break these
rules.
